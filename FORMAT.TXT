VOCABULA 0.1 TEXT FILE FORMATS
============================

LESSON.VOC
----------
Line 1:  language-name : language-name
Line 2+: synonym, synonym/synonym : synonym, synonym/synonym

Commas and forward slashes both separate synonyms. Leading and trailing
whitespace around fields is ignored. A backslash quotes the next byte, so use
\,, \/, \:, and \\ for literal punctuation. Blank lines and # comment lines are
ignored. Lines are limited to 1023 bytes; individual entries are limited to
79 bytes.

Example:

English : German
watching, looking : sehen, schauen
house/home : Haus/Zuhause
colon\: word : Doppelpunktwort

During answer checking, spaces and periods are ignored. Thus "New York",
"NewYork", and "New... York." compare as the same answer.

Leading articles and infinitive markers may be present or omitted:

the, to, der, die, das, le, la, les, l', il, lo, i, gli, el, los, las

A difference between the straight apostrophe, backtick, and DOS CP850 acute
accent is counted correct, but Vocabula shows the form stored in the lesson.

LESSON.SUM
----------
Append-only session summaries:

2026-09-15 12:30 asked=20 right=17 wrong=3 hints=2 score=26 name=Nora

Older summary lines without a name remain valid and appear as "Student".

LESSON.CAR
----------
Append-only card events. Card numbers refer to physical lesson order, starting
at one. This lets external tools calculate totals without Pauker rewriting an
existing file:

2026-09-15 12:30 card=7 result=right

LESSON.ERR
----------
Append-only wrong-answer details:

2026-09-15 12:30 card=7 question=[watching] answer=[zusehen] expected=[sehen, schauen]

Text inside square brackets is informational in version 0.2. Consumers should
use the timestamp and card number as the stable fields.
